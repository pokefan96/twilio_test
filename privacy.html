SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This policy describes how phone numbers and message data are handled for the SMS and MMS messaging operated by Aaron Steig ("we", "us"). This is a personal, non-commercial software development and testing project.

Information we collect

We collect only the mobile phone numbers that are entered directly by the account owner for the purpose of sending test messages, along with the content and delivery status of those messages. We do not collect phone numbers from any third-party source, purchased list, or public directory.

How we use information

Mobile phone numbers are used solely to send and receive the test messages described in our Terms and Conditions. Message content and delivery logs are used only to verify that the software is functioning correctly.

Sharing of information

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers or SMS consent to any third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Phone numbers are disclosed only to our messaging service provider (Twilio) and the underlying mobile carriers strictly as required to transmit the messages, and to comply with applicable law.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and is low volume. Messages are sent only when testing is actively being performed, and are typically fewer than ten messages per day.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Opt-out requests are processed automatically and immediately.

Data retention

Message logs are retained only as long as needed for testing and are deleted when no longer required. No data is used to build a profile of any individual.
